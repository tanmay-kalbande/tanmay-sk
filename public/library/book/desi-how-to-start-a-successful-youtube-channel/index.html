1. Pehla Funda: Apna Niche aur Mindset Set Kar

Sab se pehle ek sach sun. YouTube koi lottery nahi hai jis mein ticket kaat ke tumhara channel viral ho jayega. Yeh ek aisi dairy farm hai jahan tumhe roz dudh dena padega, warna bhais kisi aur ke ghar chali jayegi. Ekdin mehnat nahi karoge, agle din view nahi aayega. Bas. Itna simple hai. Zyadatar log yahan aate hain "fame" ke chakkar mein. Bhai, agar sirf followers chahiye the toh Instagram pe reel daal ke thumka lagate, wahan faster growth milega. YouTube wo jagah hai jahan audience aati hai seekhne ke liye, entertain hone ke liye, ya thodi der ke liye reality bhoolne ke liye. Tumhara kaam hai unko ek reason dena rukne ka. Agar tumhare paas wajah nahi hai, toh algorithm tumhe 10 seconds mein skip kar dega. Algorithm koi saadhu nahi hai jo tumhari mehnat dekh ke rona roye. Wo ek machine hai jo dekhti hai log kahan takke hain. Toh pehla kaam? Apna aukaat aur raasta samajh lo. Khoya Hua Kabutar Banna Band Kar Market mein aaj kal ek bimari phaili hai—har koi sab kuch banata hai. Aaj tech review, kal cooking vlog, parso morning motivation. Bhai, kya kar raha hai? Tu YouTuber hai ya swiss army knife? Soch is baare mein. Tum local train mein ho, peak hour chal raha hai. Ek aadmi chillata hai "Vada pav le lo!" Tumhe pata hai usse vada pav hi milega. Agar wo achanak se bolega "Vada pav aur laptop repair, dono yahin par ho jayega!" Toh tum usko pagal samjhega na? YouTube pe bhi same logic lagta hai. Agar tum ek din gaming stream karoge, dusre din finance ka gyaan doge, toh audience confuse ho jayegi. Algorithm ka toh dimaag ud jayega. Usko pata hi nahi rahega ki tumhara video kisse ko dikhana hai. Niche (Manchaha Khel) kya hai? Niche tumhara wo playground hai jahan tum ek specific game kheloge. Gaming, Tech, Finance, Comedy, Faceless AI videos—kuch bhi ho sakta hai. Lekin ek cheez fix honi chahiye. Tumhe decide karna hai ki tumhare channel pe aane wala banda kya expect karega. Ab ye mat sochna ki "Bhai, mera interest toh bohot cheezon mein hai." Sabka hota hai. Par YouTube pe jeet wahi jeeta hai jo ek cheez ko itna ghoonta hai ki log uss cheez ke liye usko hi yaad karein. Agar tech karna hai toh sirf smartphones ka review mat karo. Aisa kar ki sirf budget smartphones ke camera quality pe focus karo. Uss chhote se area ko apna bana lo. Takeaway: Ek haath mein sone ka sikka doosre haath mein chai ka cup? Dono gir jayenge. Ek cheez pakad, ussi pe apna naam kama. Bade Bhai Log Ki X-Ray Kaise Nikale Dare hue chup-chap corner mein baithne …

2. Machaane Ka Saamaan: Camera, Mic aur Lighting

Bina camera ke video banega kya bhai? Awaz sunai degi kya? Sab kuch andhere mein hoga? Pehli fursat mein nikal. YouTube pe aane ke liye tere paas Arjun ki aukaat nahi chahiye, bas Eklavya wala focus chahiye. Bheed hamesha uski sunti hai jo seedha point pe aata hai. Toh chhod saari bakchodi, aa apna saamaan dekhte hain. Paise paani mein daalne ki koi zaroorat nahi hai, samjha? Camera Ka Khel: iPhone Se Arri Alexa Tak Ka Safar Bhai, sabse pehla aur sabse bada bharam jo naye creators ke dimaag mein baitha hota hai, woh hai "Mehnga Camera". Dekh, 4K aana zindagi mein nahi badalta. Agar tera content kachra hai, toh 8K camera bhi tera video rescue nahi kar payega. Aaj kal ke zamaney mein tera smartphone kaafi hai. Haan, padh le. Agar tera phone 2019 ke baad ka hai, toh uska camera 1080p ya 4K pe record karta hai. Aur sach bataun toh 1080p (Full HD) hi standard hai YouTube ka. Zyada log mobile pe dekhte hain, wahan 4K aur 1080p mein zyada farq padta nahi. Real Life Example: Gali cricket khelne wale bachhon ko kabhi MRF ki bat chahiye hoti hai kya? Nahi. Woh kisi bhi faltu ke patthar ke sath apna shot maarte hain. Agar timing sahi hai, toh ball chhakke pe jaati hai. Camera tera bat hai, content tera timing. Agar phone se hi shoot kar raha hai, toh ek sasta tripod le le. Bina tripod ke handheld chalta rehta hai toh dekhne wale ko motion sickness ho jayega. Aur yaad rakh, phone ka camera kabhi bhi 60fps (frames per second) pe set kar dena. 30fps thoda cinematic lagta hai, par 60fps super smooth. Bachi hui battery aur storage ka dhyan rakhna, kyunki 4K recording phone ka bhapda jaldi uda deti hai. Baaki dSLR ya mirrorless in sab ke pichhe mat daud. Jab tak tera channel chal nahi jata, tera phone hi tera sabse acha dost hai. Takeaway: Camera sirf ek tool hai, asli jaan toh teri soch mein hai. Pehle phone se shuru, baad mein Arri Alexa! Awaaz Ki Aukaat: Bhidku Audio Pe Kabhi Compromise Nahi Karti Agar tujhe ek jagah paise bachane hain, toh camera pe bacha. Par agar tujhe ek jagah aukaat dikhani hai, toh woh hai teri AWAAZ. Dekh, agar tera video thoda blurry hai, ya lighting thodi low hai, toh bheed adjust kar legi. Par agar teri awaaz phati hui hai, ya background mein auto ki awaaz aa rahi hai, toh viewer 3 second mein video band karke scroll kar dega. Yeh pakka guarantee hai. Analogies: Soch, tu kisi date pe gaya hai. Tu kala kothi pehne hue hai, baal perfect set kiye hain, par jaise hi tu bolne laga, teri awaaz …

3. Idea Ka Khazana: Content Strategy Banao Boss

Camera aur mic toh band kar diye? Shabaash. Ab agle 5 minute soch, kya record karega? Khaali screen ko ghoorta rahega ya phir wahi ghisa-pita 'Subah Ki Routine' wala video bana ke 10 views laayega? Bhai, YouTube pe aise hi lakdo ke jhund aaye the, aate rahenge. Jo content strategy ke bina sidha record pe chadh jaata hai, woh pehle din hi digital kudali maar deta hai apne channel ki. Idea hi tera raw material hai, iske bina tu factory kya chalayega? Aaj nahi toh nahi. Aaj idea generation ka asli khel khelna hai. Khol kaan, samajh paadhai. Trending Ka Tamasha Aur Evergreen Ka Thenga Sabse pehla bakwaas jo har naye creator dimaag mein daalta hai: "Bhai, aaj kya trend kar raha hai? Upar wala video bana leta hoon!" Ruk ja. Sahi mein soch. Tu local train mein chadhta hai na rush hour mein? Jo sabse pehle bhagte wahan ghuste hain, uski halat kya hoti hai? Bheetar ghussa toh, par chhat ki pankh se chipak ke baithna padta hai. Trending content bilkul waisa hi hai. Aaj viral hua, kal algorithm ne flush maar diya. Views aayenge, par subscribers? Woh temporary honge. Kal koi aur trend aaya, sab bhool jayenge tera naam. Dusri taraf, evergreen content hai. Yeh woh vada pav ka thela hai jo station ke bahar 20 saal se laga hai. Subah ek aadmi khaata hai, raat ko ek aur. Trend nahi marta, par sale roze hoti hai. Search volume aaram se chalta rehta hai saalo tak. 'How to tie a tie' wali video dekhi hai? Us bande ne 2012 mein daali thi, aaj bhi log usko search kar ke dekhte hain. Uski aukaat alag hi ban gayi. Par sirf evergreen pe chal ke tu Ambani nahi banega. Algorithm tujhe push karega achanak, tabhi tera channel rocket ki tarah chalega. Isliye, sambhal ke chal. Trending tujhe initial boost deta hai, evergreen tera ghar chalata hai. Agar tu sirf trend ke peeche bhagega, toh tu kabhi apna identity nahi bana payega. Trending pe bhagega, evergreen pe rahega. Aukaat Aur Dard: Audience Ka X-Ray Abe oy, tu kiske liye bana raha hai video? "Sabke liye!" — yeh jawab de toh tera channel pehle din hi doob jayega. Sabke liye banaya toh kisi ke kaam ka nahi rahega. Gali cricket khelte the na bachpan mein? Ek aadmi sabse chota bat maarta hai, ek sabse lamba six. Pitch aur bowler ke hisaab se khelna padta hai. YouTube pe bhi same scene hai. Tujhe apne viewer ka 'pain point' dhoondhna hai. Usko kya takleef hai? Tu us takleef ka ilaaj deta hai, tera video chal jayega. Kisi college ke first-year student ki pain point kya hai? Ragging ka darr, attendance kaise manage karein, ya …

4. Bhookh Lagi Hai?: Scripting aur Hooking Ka Khel

Scene set kar. Camera on hai. Ring light teri aakhein phod raha hai. Tu script padhne wala hai jo tune kal raat 2 baje type ki thi. "Hello guys, welcome to my channel, aaj hum baat karenge..." Bhenchod, yeh bolte hi viewer ka remote daba aur tu waahin pe bye-bye. YouTube par attention span ek machhar se bhi kam hai. Machhar kam se kam khoon toh nikaalta hai, tera viewer seedha 'Next Video' par swipe maar deta hai. Agar teri video ke pehle 30 second logon ko pakad nahi paaye, toh baaki ki 15 minute ki mehnat ki tu apne ghar ki mummy ko dikha dena. Algorithm ko farak nahi padta. Isliye, aaj scripting aur hooking ki aisi class hogi ki tera next video dekh kar logon ka dimaag freeze ho jaye. Ready? Chal dhyan se padh. Pehle 30 Second Ka Atank: The Hook Soch, tu local train pakadne gaya hai Virar fast. Train aayi, darwaza khula. Tere paas sirf 3 second hain andar ghusne ke liye. Agar tu lele lag gaya, toh agle station tak tu platform pe khada 'Bhaag Milkha Bhaag' ki shooting kar raha hoga. YouTube ka hook bhi aisi hi local train hai. Tu 30 second ke andar viewer ko andar nahi ghusayega, toh woh bhaag jayega. Log "Hello guys" se shuru karte hain. Yeh sabse bada chutiyapa hai. Viewer tujhe jaanta nahi hai, usko tera naam sun kar koi farak nahi padta. Usko farak padta hai uski problem se. Real Life Example: Tu kabhi vada pav khaane gaya hai kisi naye thele pe? Kya woh wala bolta hai, "Namaste, mera naam Suresh hai, main pichle 10 saal se vada pav bana raha hoon"? Nahi boss! Woh seedha bolta hai, "Bhaiya, lasun chutney aag lagane wali hai, try kar!" Tera muh mein paani aa jata hai aur tu khareed leta hai. Tera hook bhi wahi lasun chutney hona chahiye. Seedha point. Hook Banane Ke 3 Tarike: 1. Curiosity Drop: "Maine apne pichle video mein ek galti ki jo mujhe 10,000 views ka nuksaan de gayi..." (Viewer sochega, "Arre, kya hua? Chal dekh lete hain.") 2. Pain Point Uthao: "Tera phone hang ho raha hai aur tu usko patak raha hai? Ruk, ek minute de." (Viewer relate karega.) 3. Result Seedha Dikhao: "Aaj ke video ke baad tera Wi-Fi 3x fast chalega, guarantee." (Value bomb.) Jo bhi kare, pehle 10 second mein viewer ko bata ki uska time yahan barbaad nahi jayega. Takeaway: "Hello guys" bolna apne pairo par kulhadi maarne jaisa hai. Seedha value de, baad mein naam bol. Kachori Ka Khoka: Script Ka Structure Soch le, tu ek gali cricket khel raha hai. Pehle ball tu aise phenkega ki batsman soch raha hoga yeh wide …

5. Rolling Camera: Shoot aur Edit Kaise Kare

Record button dabaane se pehle khud se puch: "Kya main ekdum tayaar hoon?" Agar andar se aawaz aaye "Haan", toh seedha aaage badh. Camera on karo, mic check kar lo, aur khel shuru karo. Frame Ka Khel: Screen Ko Mat Bhara Screen ke beech mein khada hoke bolna shuru kar diya? Bhai, tu YouTube video bana raha hai ya passport size photo khinchwa raha hai? Screen ke upar neeche itni khaali jagah chhodta hai ki lagta hai tera face house arrest pe hai. Frame composition ka matlab hai ki tera subject (yaani tu) screen par kaisa dikhega. Ekdum center mein hone ke chakkar mein video boring lagti hai. Yahan aata hai sabse bada rule: Rule of Thirds. Bhai, agar tu cricket khelta hai toh pata hoga off-stump kahan hota hai. Waise hi frame ko 9 equal boxes mein divide kar le. Do horizontal lines, do vertical lines. Tere chehre ki aankhein ideally usi upper horizontal line par aani chahiye. Tujhe screen ke left ya right side pe thoda shift hona chahiye, beech mein nahi. Isse viewer ki nazar naturally teri taraf khinchti hai aur background mein thoda space bhi dikhta hai jo visual depth deta hai. Agar tu vada pav kha raha hai aur camera bilkul uske upar se shoot kar raha hai, toh viewer ko ghutan hogi. Camera thoda side pe rakh, angle mein la. Visuals ka apna ek flow hota hai. Samjha kya? Takeaway: Frame ko beech mein nahi, thirds mein baant. Center mein khade hone ka trend chhod, screen par jagah bana. Headroom aur Leading Space Ka Chakkar Headroom matlab tera sir aur frame ke top ke beech ka gap. Agar zyada gap hai, toh tu screen par ek chota sa keeda lagta hai. Agar bilkul nahi gap hai, toh lagta hai tera sir frame ke bahar nikal jayega. Ekdum perfect balance chahiye. Aur ha, agar tu screen ke left side khada hai aur bol raha hai, toh tere dekhne ki direction mein zyada space honi chahiye (Leading Space). Warnu lagta hai tu deewar se baat kar raha hai. Are you getting this? Chhutti Kar Bhai: Bloopers Aur Khaali Jagah Ka Safaya "Umm... acha sun na... main basically... woh... acha chhod." Yeh sab sun ke viewer apna aadhi umar phone mein ghusa lega. Editing ka sabse bada aur pehla kaam hai: Bhaad mein bhejo un lamhon ko jo tere video ki speed ko roko. Gali cricket khelte waqt agar koi player overthrows maare, toh usko bahar baitha dete hain na? Waise hi editing table pe tere faltu ke pauses, "umm", "aah", aur galat bolke phir se theek se bolne ke attempts—sab ko cut karna hai. Video ko crisp rakhna hai. Silence ya dead air video ka sabse …

6. Pakad Lo Bhai: Thumbnail aur Title Ka Jadoo

Soch, tu ne raat bhar mehnat ki, script ka achaar daala, editing mein aankhein faad li. Video ekdum blockbuster ban gaya. Upload kiya, soch raha hai "ab toh bawal machega!"... par views 12 pe atak gaye, aur usme se 4 tera hi WhatsApp pe bhejne wala view hai. Bhai, tere video mein fault nahi hai, teri 'Dukaan ki Board' bekar hai. Jab koi bande ko tera video feed mein dikhta hai, toh usko tere andar ki mehnat nahi dikhti. Usko sirf do cheezein dikhti hain—Ek choti si image (Thumbnail) aur ek line (Title). Agar yeh dono ne uski aankh nahi pakdi, toh tera video kachre ke dabbe jaisa hai. YouTube par logo ka attention span ek machhar se bhi kam hai. Tu chahe Bhagat Singh ki biography bana le, agar thumbnail bekar hua toh koi nahi dekhta. Isliye chup-chaap dhyan se sun, aaj tere dukaan ki naliyan saaf karni hain. Thumbnail Ka Khel: Sabse Pehle Aankh Pakdo Thumbnail tera digital vada pav ka thela hai. Mumbai ke beach par 50 thele hain, bande ko kya khana hai woh thele ki safai aur uspe lage signboard se pata chalta hai. Tera thumbnail sirf ek kaam karna hai—ruko, dekho, aur click karo. Beginner log ek sabse bada chutiyapa karte hain. Video ka best scene ka screenshot nikalte hain, uspe uska khud ka title text daal dete hain, aur thoda sa blur karke thumbnail bana lete hain. Bhai, yeh toh woh baat hui ki dukaan pe laga diya "Yahan dukaan hai", aur andar aake pata chale kya milta hai. Thumbnail ka kaam info dena nahi hai, thumbnail ka kaam hai curiosity paida karna. Agar thumbnail pe poori kahani bata di, toh log padh ke chale jayenge. Video kaun dekhega? Samjha kya? Ek rule yaad rakh: Title batata hai kya hai, thumbnail batata hai kya hone wala hai. Honest Clickbait Ka Funda Clickbait aakhir hai kya? Woh ek lollipop hai jo mooh mein daalte hi pata chal jaye ki flavour aam hai. Par clickbait galat nahi hota jab tak wo jhooth nahi bol raha. Agar tu video mein 3 baat batane wala hai, toh thumbnail mein sirf aadhi baat dikhao. Baaki aadhi video ke andar khulegi. Jaise, agar tune video banayi "₹1000 se ₹10,000 kaise banaye", toh thumbnail pe mat likh "Step by step guide". Thumbnail pe badi badi font mein daal: "₹1000 = ₹10,000?". Chota sa question mark laga. Banda sochega, "Kaise bhai? Koi Jadu hai kya?" Aur uski ungli click par jaa padegi. Par dhyaan rakh, agar tune thumbnail pe dikha diya ki 1 lakh rupaye de rahe hain, aur video mein bola "sirf 10 rupaye milte hain", toh woh fake hai. Log turant video chhod denge, YouTube algorithm samajh jayega ki …

7. Algorithm Ka Baba: SEO aur Discoverability

Abe sun, ek kaan pakad ke ek kaan se daaru pi wali feeling mat rakh apne channel ke aage. Thumbnail aur title ka game toh bant hai, par bhai yeh sab packaging hai. Asli maal woh hai jo andar rakhta hai—aur uss maal ko logon tak pahunchane wala postman hai tera YouTube Algorithm. Algorithm koi syaana baba nahi hai jo teri bhakti dekh ke views dega. Yeh ek pure number-crunching machine hai. Isko bas do cheezein chahiye: "Khaana" (Data) aur "Satisfying Customers" (Audience Retention). Tu isko sahi data feed karega, yeh tera video har gali ke nuke par pahuncha dega. Nahi kiya toh tera 100 ghante ka edit karke daalo internet ke andheré mein. Chal, is algorithm ke nakhre samajhte hain aur SEO ka asli khel khelte hain. Algorithm Ki Aukaat Aur Tera Kaam Log bolte hain "YouTube algorithm mere video ko dabaa raha hai." Arey chup kar pagle! Algorithm ke paas tujhse koi dushmani nahi hai. Yeh ek auto-wala hai. Agar tu usko seedha raasta batayega (Keywords), aur uske andar baithne wale passenger ko AC aur gaana pasand aayega (Content), toh woh tujhe destination (Views) tak pahunchayega. Algorithm ka ek hi master hai: The Viewer. Jo dekh raha hai, woh kya search kar raha hai? Kya dekh ke ruk raha hai? Yeh sab data YouTube ke servers mein jaata hai. Agar tera video kisi bande ko 10 second mein bore karke bhaga de, toh algorithm samajh jayega ki bhai is dukaan ka maal bekaar hai. Phir woh tera video kisi aur ko recommend nahi karega. Real Life Example: Local train ki bhagdad samajh. Subah 9 baje Virar se Churchgate ki train mein agar tu khaali seat dekhega toh pichhe se 50 log tujhe dhakka maar ke baith jayenge. Algorithm wahi bhagdad wali crowd hai. Agar teri video (train) unke destination (interest) ki nahi hai, ya andar jagah nahi hai (value nahi hai), toh log utar jayenge. Retention girega, views girenge. Bhai, algorithm tere video ko pehle hi 'test' karta hai. Ek chhota sa audience group ko dikhata hai. Agar woh group video click karta hai aur dekhta hai, toh algorithm bada group ko dikhata hai. Isko 'Impressions' aur 'Click-Through Rate (CTR)' bolte hain. Click mila? Video dekha? Niche aa. Takeaway: Algorithm dushman nahi, tera auto-wala hai. Meter par data aur audience retention chalta hai. Search Ka Khel: Log Kya Maang Rahe Hain? YouTube duniya ka sabse bada search engine hai Google ke baad. Matlab? Matlab log yahan aake search karke videos dekhte hain. Agar tu search ke game mein aa gaya, toh tera 'Evergreen' content aane wale 3 saal tak paisa kamaayega. Search mein aane ke liye tujhe sochna padega ek aam insaan ki tarah. Tu gym jata hai? …

8. Aukaat Dikhana: Upload, Analytics aur Consistency

Upload button dabaate hi YouTube par koi jadoo nahi hota. Algorithm tujhe dekh raha hai. Tere dwara banaye gaye pichle 7 chapters ke effort ka asli test ab hoga. Thumbnail click hua ya nahi? Video dekhi gayi ya bounce kar diya? Yeh sab tujhe YouTube Studio bataayega. Is chapter mein hum baat karenge upload karne ke sahi tareeke, analytics ko padhne ke baare mein aur burnout se bachte hue ek consistent schedule maintain karne ke baare mein. Seedha point pe aate hain. Upload Ka Khel: Time, Date Aur Nautanki Bhai, video ban gayi. Edit ho gayi. Thumbnail ready hai. Ab seedha upload button daba ke public kar diya? Ruk ja thoda. Local train mein jab bhag ke chadhta hai, toh dekhta hai kaunsi seat khali hai, right? Wahi logic YouTube pe bhi apply hota hai. Tere viewers ka ek routine hota hai. Agar tu Monday subah 9 baje upload karega jab tera audience office ki traffic mein phasa hai ya local train ke dabe mein sandwich ho raha hai, toh tere views pe kya hoga? Dhakke padenge. Best Time To Upload Kya Hai? YouTube ka algorithm pehle 24 ghante dekhta hai ki teri video pe kitna action ho raha hai. Agar teri audience online nahi hai, toh woh initial push nahi milega. Dhyan rakh, beginner hain tu. Abhi tera channel naya hai. YouTube tujhe exact data de raha hoga "When your viewers are on YouTube" ka. Studio ke 'Analytics' tab mein ja, 'Audience' select kar. Wahan teri audience ke active hone ka time graph milega. Jab log online hote hain, usse 1-2 ghante pehle upload kar. Kyun? Kyunki jab video publish hoti hai, YouTube usko thoda index karta hai, thumbnails render karta hai. Agar tu unke aane ke theek pehle upload karega, toh jab tera audience login karega, teri video unke feed mein fresh hogi. Schedule Kar Ya Seedha Public? Agar tu ek consistent banda hai (jo tu hona chahiye), toh hamesha schedule kar. Ek date fix kar, ek time fix kar. Isse teri audience ko pata hota hai ki "bhai yeh aadmi har Shanivaar shaam 6 baje aata hai". Unka dimaag teri channel ke liye train ho jata hai. Unpredictable upload karne wale creators ko log jaldi bhool jaate hain. Pakka schedule hai tera aukaat. Punchline: Aukaat tab aati hai jab tu algorithm ko bataaye ki kya karna hai, na ki algorithm tujhe bataye. Data Ki Aukaat: YouTube Studio Ka Post-Mortem Video upload kar di, aur phir phone rakh ke chai peene chala gaya? Yeh galti hai. Bade creators aur tere mein farak yahi hai ki woh apni video ke baad ke data ko date ki tarah dekhte hain. Har move, har second ka hisaab rakhte hain. YouTube Studio …

9. Paisa Hi Paisa: Monetization aur Brand Deals

Tube light jalne ka time aa gaya hai. Ab tak tu thumbnails banata raha, SEO ka jaadu seekha, aur analytics mein ghoonta lagaya. Par chakkar yeh hai—bill toh electricity ka aata hai, server ka aata hai, internet ka aata hai. Tu jab tak paisa nahi kamayega, tab tak yeh sab ek expensive hobby ban ke rahega. YouTube ek aurat nahi hai jo paise degi, yeh ek system hai. Is system ko crack kar, toh aukaat ke hisaab se notes khud chal ke aayenge. Baat karte hain directly paise ki—kyunki "Paisa Hi Paisa" wala angle ab activate karna hai. AdSense aur YPP: Nautanki Band, Pehla Kamai Ka Ticket Aapne YouTube pe 1000 subs aur 4000 hours watch time poore kar liye? Mubarak ho, ab tu minimum wage ke kareeb hai. Yeh threshold YPP (YouTube Partner Program) ka basic rule hai. Iske bina tu ad revenue (AdSense) se ek rupee nahi kheench sakta. Par ek naya rule bhi aaya hai—Shorts ka. Agar tera Shorts game strong hai, toh 10 million views (90 days ke andar) laana padega YPP ke liye. Long-form ya Shorts, dono ka apna criteria hai. Jo bhi route chun, target poora karna padega. Koi shortcut nahi hai bhai, local train ka platform pakad ke train rok nahi sakte, andar ghusna padta hai. Yahan bhi system ke andar ghusna padega. AdSense Ka Khel: Eklauti Aukaat Nahi Hai AdSense ka funda simple hai—video ke beech mein ad chalta hai, advertiser paise deta hai, YouTube apna 45% cut rakhta hai, baaki 55% tujhe milta hai. Par yeh mat sochna ki 1000 views aaye toh 1000 rupaye milenge. Ekdum nahi. Yeh CPM (Cost Per Mille) aur RPM (Revenue Per Mille) pe depend karta hai. - CPM: Advertiser kitne dollars de raha hai 1000 views ke liye. - RPM: Tujhe kitne dollars mil rahe hain 1000 views ke liye (YouTube ke cut ke baad). Agar tera channel finance ya tech ka hai, toh RPM high jayega. Agar roasting ya prank wala content hai, toh advertisers thoda peeche hatte hain, RPM low aayega. Pakka number koi nahi bata sakta, niche pe depend karta hai. Bas itna samajh, views hi sab kuch nahi hote, kis topic pe views aaye, yeh matter karta hai. Takeaway: AdSense se shuruati paise milte hain, par isko apni aukaat mat samajhna. Yeh bas trailer hai, asli picture abhi baaki hai.
