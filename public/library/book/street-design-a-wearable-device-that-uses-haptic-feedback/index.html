Feels like your device is having a seizure. - Intensity (G): Measured in G-forces. Too weak? Feels like a ghost tapping your shoulder. Too strong? Feels like a woodpecker jackhammering your wrist. 💡 Pro Tip: Your brain processes haptic feedback faster than visual or auditory cues.
