If you can’t tie an expense directly to revenue within a month, don’t spend it. - The Float: Get your customers to pay you before you have to pay your suppliers. Subscription models? Pre-orders? Hell yes. "Net 30" terms? Hell no. - The Emergency Stash: Keep 3 months of operating expenses in cash. No, your credit line doesn’t count. If you don’t have this, you’re not ready to scale. ⚠️ Common Mistake: "I’ll just raise more money." Cute. Investors aren’t your personal ATM.
