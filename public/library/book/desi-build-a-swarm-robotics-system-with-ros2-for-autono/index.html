Chal shuru. --- Asli Funda (Core Concepts) 1. Boids aur Artificial Potential Field (APF) - Bhed Ka Mental Model Dekh bhai, ye coordination ka pehla rule hai: "Akele mat nikal, aur akele bhi mat reh." Iske do main models hain: Boids aur APF. Boids (Reynolds Rules): Ye 1987 ka model hai. Isme robot ko sirf teen rules yaad hote hain, bas. 1. Separation: Apne aas paas ke robots se door reh. (Jaise local train mein log apna bag dabakar thoda space banate hain). 2. Alignment: Apne neighbour ki speed aur direction pe match kar. (Jaise traffic signal green hone pe sab ek sath aage badhte hain). 3. Cohesion: Bheed ke center ki taraf jhuk. (Jaise tapri pe chai peene wale sab ek jagah gathbandhan karte hain). Artificial Potential Field (APF): Ye thoda physics ka rasta hai. Samajh, robot ke charo taraf ek invisible magnetic field hai. - Apne team ke robots ke beech mein "Repulsive force" (hatane wala taakat) — taaki takkar na ho. - Target (victim) pe "Attractive force" (kheenchne wala taakat) — taaki goal tak pahunche. - Obstacles pe "Repulsive force" — taaki deewar se na takraye. 💡 Pro Tip: Boids perfect hai formation aur flocking ke liye.
