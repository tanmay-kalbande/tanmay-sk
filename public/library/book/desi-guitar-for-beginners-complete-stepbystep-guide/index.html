1. Apna Ustaad Pehchano: Guitar Khareedo aur Samjho

Seedha baat pe aate hain. Guitar sikhni hai? Pehle apne hathiyar ko pehchan. Local market mein chowmein khaane gaya tha aur wahan se jo bhi dukan wale ne 2000 rupaye mein "professional guitar" thama diya, woh abhi tak cupboards mein sadak raha hai. Aaj hum usi bakchodi ko khatam karenge. Guitar ek machine hai. Agar tujhe machine ke parts nahi pata, toh tu engine mein daal barabar karega aur phir rona nahi chahiye. Hum yahan theory ka kachra nahi phailayenge, par jo cheez zaroori hai, woh itne faad ke samjhayenge ki bhoolna mushkil ho jaye. Ready? Chal dhande pe chalte hain. Shaant Sipahi ya Bijli Ka Bhoot: Apna Rang Pehchano Guitar ke dukaan pe gaya, toh do type ke lakde phate hue dikhte hain: Ek Acoustic, ek Electric. Naya aadmi sochta hai "chalo sasta wala lete hain, baad mein electric le lenge." Yahi pe log phas jate hain. Dono ki alag aukaat hai, alag mizaaj hai. Acoustic: Apne Aap Mein Poora Acoustic guitar woh hai jisme bina kisi current ya amplifier ke awaaz aati hai. Iska dabba (body) bada hota hai kyunki yehi dabba awaaz ko andar goonjata hai. Iska kaam hai khud se awaaz dena. Yeh tere ghar ka inverter hai, bijli jaye toh bhi chalta hai. Agar tujhe chai ke tapri pe baith ke gaana bajana hai, ya akele room mein gungunane hain, toh yeh tera banda hai. Isme steel ke patle taar (strings) hote hain jo ungliyon ko thoda kaatte hain shuru mein, par yahi taar tere fingernails ko steel banate hain. Electric: Bijli Ka Jhatka Electric guitar chuppi rehti hai bina current ke. Ise bolte hain "amplifier" wale bade dabbe mein ghusedna padta hai, tabhi iski aukaat saamne aati hai. Iska body solid hoti hai, patli hoti hai. Taar acoustically awaaz nahi karte, balki iske andar 'pickups' hote hain jo string ke vibration ko electrical signal mein badalte hain. Electric sikhne ka faayda? Yeh bahut soft hoti hai. Ungli se string dabana bacchon ka khel hai isme. Awaaz ke 1000 types nikal sakte hain isme. Nuksaan? Tu iske piche lagatar paise lagayega—cables, amp, effects pedals. Yeh woh girlfriend hai jo maintenance mangti hai. Faisla Kaise Le? Dekh, agar tu bilkul zero se shuru kar raha hai aur tujhe sirf gaana bajana hai, toh Acoustic le le. Agar tujhe rock/metal ka bhoot sawar hai aur tu chilla ke bolta hai "main lead guitarist banunga", toh seedha electric utha le. Par yaad rakh, electric ke bina amp ek lakdi ka tukda hai. Takeaway: Acoustic akela kharcha nahi leta, electric paisa khaata hai. Apni jeb tol aur pasand pehle, phir hawa se baat. Lakdi Ka Jhaad: Anatomy Ka Khel Guitar ko utha. Yeh sirf ek lakdi nahi hai, iske …

2. Sur Sangeet: Thaamo Sahi aur Tune Karo

Seedha kaam pe aate hain. Guitar haath mein hai, par kya karu iska? Kachra pakad ke baitha hai kya tu? Bina posture aur tuning ke guitar bajana, woh bhi andhe kooke restaurant mein bina menu deke order dena hai. Jo nikla wahi khila dega. Aaj tere is kachre ko sone ka haar pehnaayenge. Posture se lekar tuning tak, sab kuch breakdown karenge. Ready? Chal blast karte hain. Khamba Bano: Posture aur Pakad Rehna Ka Tareeka Sabse pehla kaam: apne aap ko dekh mirror mein. Guitar ko jaise koi sanyasi baingan pakadta hai, waise mat pakad. Back teri dukhni chahiye, guitar nahi. Aise baith ki jo aage wala dekhe, woh samjhe tu kisi rockstar ka chhota bhai hai, kisi genda (rhinoceros) ka nahi. Sahi Baithne Ka Funda: Kursi pe aadha-aadha baith. Full nahi. Dono pair flat zameen par. Guitar ki body teri right ya left taang ke upar aayegi (jo teri strumming hand hai). Guitar ka neck thoda upar, 30-40 degree angle pe. Neck neeche jhukega nahi. Jhuka toh haath slip padega aur chords galti bajenge. Haath Ka Setup: Strumming wala haath (right ya left jo bhi dominant hai) guitar ke soundhole/body ke upar set hoga. Fretboard wala haath neck ko pakdega, par "death grip" mat maar. Guitar ko sirf ungliyon se pakadna hai. Haath ka thumb neck ke peeche rest karega. Aisa soch le jaise tu kisi chhori ka haath politely pakad raha hai, na ki kisi kida ko maarne ke liye daba raha hai. Real Life Example: Local train ka crowded dabba yaad rakh. Jab tu khada hota hai, toh balance maintain karne ke liye tu seedha khada rehta hai, jhukta nahi. Waise hi guitar ka neck bhi seedha aur thoda upar rehna chahiye. Agar neck neeche jhukega, tera balance bigad jayega aur chord bajate time haath atak jayega. Takeaway: Seedha baith, guitar ko apne shareer se chipka, aur neck ko thoda ooncha rakh. Pakad pakad ke nahi, balance se. Kachra Pakad Ke Chhod: Tuning Pegs Ka Khel Ab aate hain uss hisse pe jo sabse zyada log kharab karte hain. Tuning pegs. Yeh woh pahiye (wheels) hain jo headstock pe lage hote hain. Inko ghuma ke tu strings ko tight ya loose karta hai, jisse sur (pitch) badalta hai. Pegs ko ghumaana koi pahalwan ka kaam nahi hai. Tu koi nut-bolt nahi khol raha. Halka, chhota sa movement kaafi hota hai. Tuning Pegs Ka Mechanic: - Agar peg ko ghumaane se string tight hoti hai, toh sur ooncha (higher pitch) jata hai. - Agar peg ko ghumaane se string dheela hoti hai, toh sur neeche (lower pitch) aata hai. Analogy: Tuning peg ko ghar ka paani wala tap (faucet) samajh le. Thoda sa ghumaaya, paani (sur) aane laga. …

3. Pehla Wanda: Open Chords ki Shuruat

Unglees phat jaayengi, dard hoga, aawaz buzzer-buzzer aayegi—lekin yeh sab normal hai. Aaj hum us safar ki shuruwat kar rahe hain jahan tu ek 'guitar uthane wala' se 'guitar bajane wala' banega. Pehle hi chapter mein humne posture aur haath set kiya tha. Ab time hai strings ko dabaane ka. Direct point pe aate hain. Aaj hum 8 basic open chords ke baare mein baat karenge. Yeh aath chords teri guitar ki aarti-pooja hain. Inke bina koi gaana nahi baja. Daba Ke Rakho Boss: Fingers aur Fretboard Ka Connection Strings dabana aasan lagta hai, lekin 90% beginners isme hi ghoti kha jaate hain. Strings ko dabane ki ek science hai. Agar tu finger kahin bhi rakh dega, toh guitar tera mooh laal karegi. Buzzing (buzzi-buzzi aawaz) niklegi aur motivation gir jayega. Fretboard pe dekh. Woh metal ki lines hain na? Unko 'Frets' kehte hain. Rule simple hai: Tere finger ko fret ke bilkul peeche nahi, thoda aage (towards the next fret) rakhna hai. Agar tu finger ko ekdum metal wire ke peeche chipkaega, toh aawaz dead ho jayegi. Agar bohot door rakhega, toh string kaampne ka space nahi milega aur phir se buzz. Desi Example: Local train ki bheed bhari hui hai. Tu gate pe khada hai. Agar tu ekdum gate ke chipak ke khada hai, toh koi andar nahi ja sakta (dead sound). Agar tu bohot peeche khada hai, toh log tujhe dhakka maar ke gate se gira denge (buzz). Tujhe bilkul center mein, ekdum perfect spot pakadna hai jahan tujhe support mile aur aage wale ko bhi jagah mile. Yeh perfect finger placement hai. Finger Arch: Yeh sabse bada problem hai. Beginners apne fingers ko flat karke strings pe rakh dete hain. "Pyaaz kaatne wali" tarah. Bhai, guitar pyaaz nahi hai. Tere fingers ek 'arch' banane chahiye. Jaise typing karte waqt keyboard pe fingers 90 degree pe hote hain. Agar finger flat hua, toh woh aas-paas ki strings ko dabayega aur aawaz phatt jayegi. Sirf fingertip se string ko chhoona hai. Ungliyon ki naram skin nahi, thodi hard skin (fingertip ka upper part) use karni hai. Takeaway: Finger arch rakho, fret ke thoda aage dabao. String ko dabana nahi, fret ke against 'press' karna hai. Pehla Wanda: Major aur Minor Ka Khel Guitar pe 8 basic chords hote hain jo har gaane ki jaan hain. Hum inhe 'Open Chords' kehte hain kyunki inme open strings (bina dabaye hue strings) bhi bajti hain. Chords do tarah ke hote hain: Major aur Minor. - Major: Khushi, masti, energy. (C, G, D, A, E) - Minor: Udaas, serious, emotional. (Am, Em, Dm) Yeh theory mein mat phas. Bas itna samajh le ki jab koi gaana khushi ka hai, toh major chords …

4. Hath Ghuma Boss: Strumming aur Rhythm

Seedha point pe aate hain. Teri left hand ne abhi tak chords thame hue hain, par right hand agar bekaar ki tarah dhol kiye ja rahi hai, toh koi nahi nachega. Guitar bajana sirf chord dabane ka khel nahi hai, asli magic isi right hand mein hota hai. Ise Hath Ghuma Boss bolte hain. Strumming aur rhythm—yahi woh cheez hai jo tujhe sirf ek "chord dabaane wale" se ek "actual musician" mein convert karti hai. Ready ho? Chal shuru karte hain. Pick Ki Pakad: Kachhua Mat Ban Plectrum (ya simply 'pick') ek chhoti si plastic ki cheez hai, par isko pakadne ka tareeka teri puri sound ka faisla karta hai. Zyadatar naye log isko itna tightly pakadte hain ki ungliyan safed (white) pad jaati hain. Bhai, guitar ka pick hai, kisi ki collar nahi. Itna tight mat pakad ki uski aatma hi nikal jaaye. Agar pick ko muthi mein band karke ghumaega, toh sound ekdum stiff aayegi. Jaise kisi ne danda maarke baja diya ho. Halka sa rehna. Pick ko thumb aur index finger ke beech rakho, baaki teen ungliyan naturally curl hoke haath ke andar rehne do. Imagine kar tere haath mein ek chhota sa kagaz ka tukda hai jo udne wala hai, par tu usko control kar raha hai. Looseness hi tujhe flow degi. Stiff haath se strum karna Mumbai ke peak hour traffic mein gear shift karne jaisa hai—har second lagta hai. Takeaway: Pick ko loose rakh, sound ko saans lene de. Tight pakad, bekar aawaz. Neeche-Upar Ka Khel: Downstroke aur Upstroke Strumming koi random hath ghumaane ka khel nahi hai. Iska ek system hai. Do basic movements hoti hain: Downstroke aur Upstroke. Downstroke: Seedha upar se neeche ki taraf ghumaana. Isko hum 'D' se denote karte hain. Yeh heavy aur strong sound deta hai. Jab bhi kisi beat ka emphasis (zor) chahiye, downstroke aati hai. Upstroke: Neeche se upar ki taraf laana. Isko 'U' bolte hain. Yeh light hoti hai. Ismein zor nahi, bas flow hota hai. Upstroke mein sirf unche strings (1st, 2nd, 3rd) touch hote hain, baaki sab chhoot jate hain. Isi wajah se yeh halki sound deti hai. Galti yahan hoti hai ki naye banda downstroke aur upstroke mein same amount of zor lagata hai. Bhai, local train mein andar aane wale logon aur bahar nikalne wale logon mein farq hota hai na? Downstroke wale log hain jo push karte hain (heavy), upstroke wale hain jo nikal rahe hain (light). Agar dono mein same zor lagaega, toh rhythm ekdum robotic lagega. Punchline: Neeche aana heavy, upar jaana light. Yeh balance hi asli groove hai. Ginti Ka Jugaad: 4/4 Beat Samajh Rhythm ko samajhne ke liye tujhe thoda maths wala hisaab karna hoga. Par …

5. Chakka Maar: Chords Change Karna Bina Ruke

Pehla chord pakad liya, doosra pakad liya... par beech mein guitar aise ruki jaise Mumbai local mein bheed ke beech phasa hua aadmi. Strumming chal rahi hai dha-n-dha-n, aur tu chord change karte time air bana raha hai. Bhai, suniyega, gaana bajana hai ya morchha phodni hai? Agar tu ruk ruk ke chord badal raha hai, toh rhythm ka tu baap bhi nahi bacha sakta. Guitar mein asli game hai transition ki. Ek chord se doosre chord mein aana aisa smooth hona chahiye jaise butter garam tave par pighle. Is chapter mein hum wahi seekhenge—bina ek second ruke, bina rhythm biggadaye chord change karna. Ready? Chalo, hath sambhaal. Muscles Ko Chhichlana: Mind Se Nahi, Ungliyon Se Bajana Tune pehle chapter mein open chords seekh liye hain. Ab dimaag ye jaanta hai ki 'C major' kaisa hota hai, aur 'G major' kaisa. Par jab strumming start karta hai, toh teri ungliyan dimaag ka order sunne ke liye ready nahi hoti. Unhe alag se training deni padti hai. Isko bolte hain Muscle Memory. Soch is baare mein: Tu cycle chalana seekhta hai. Pehle din tu dimaag se har movement sochta hai—left pedal, balance, right pedal. Par ek week baad, tu cycle par baith kar phone pe reel dekh raha hai aur cycle chal rahi hai. Guitar bhi bilkul wahi hai. Tere dimaag ko chord shape yaad hai, par tere haath ko apna banaana padega. Gali Cricket Ka Example: Gali cricket mein jab pehli baar koi bhaiya bowling karta hai, toh usko dimaag mein hota hai—"run-up le, hath utha, gend chhod". Par jab tu sachin ya virat ko dekhta hai, toh unka body ek natural flow mein hota hai. Unhe sochna nahi padta. Unka muscle memory itna strong hota hai ki ball chhutne se pehle unka bat swing ho jata hai. Guitar par bhi yahi hona chahiye. Tujhe 'A minor' se 'C major' jaate time sochna nahi chahiye ki "ab pehli ungli uthani hai, teesri rakhni hai". Tere fingers automatically wahan chhap jayne chahiye. Iske liye kya karein? Dimaag ka circuit break. Jab tu practice kare, toh chord shape banaane mein time lagega. Shuru mein 5 second bhi lag jaye toh lagne de. Par ek baar shape ban gaya, toh usko tightly pakad. Fir dheere se ungliyan utha, aur wapas same shape par wapas rakh. Utha, rakh. Utha, rakh. Ye drill 10-15 baar kar bina strumming kiye. Isse teri ungliyan us shape ko apni aadat bana lengi. Takeaway: Dimaag sirf pehli baar chord sikhata hai. Baaki zindagi muscle memory bajati hai. Isliye dimaag ko side mein rakh, ungliyon ko bhooka maar. 6. Cipher Padho Bhai: Guitar Tabs aur Chord Charts

Guitar ke peeche padi aadhi aabad duniya ko lagta hai ki chords aur strumming seekh gaye toh bas stage pe koodne ki baari hai. Bhai, agar tu sirf chords bajata raha, toh tu ek auto-rickshaw hai—thik-thak chal jayega, kaam nikal jayega. Par agar tu riffs, solos, aur woh kamaal ke hooks bajana chahta hai jo seedha dil mein utar jaaye, toh tujhe guitar ki secret language padhni padegi. Yeh chapter hai tere andar ka decoder khoolne ka. Tabs aur Chord Charts—yeh do cheezein hain jo tujhe internet ke aadhi duniya se jod deti hain. Toh chal, bina time waste kiye, cipher padhna shuru karte hain. Secret Language: Tabs Ka Structure Sheet music padhna ekdum kalesh hai. Woh saare saap jaise lines aur dots dekh ke aadhe log guitar bech ke so jaate hain. Guitar world mein humara apna cheat code hai—Tablature (ya simply, Tabs). Tab ek pure visual map hai. Soch le tere paas ek photo hai guitar ke fretboard ka, jise seedha tere saamne rakh diya gaya hai. Tabs mein 6 lines hoti hain. Ab dhyan se sun: yeh lines represent karti hain guitar ki strings ko. Lekin yahan ek chakkar hai. Jab tu guitar hold karta hai aur neeche dekhta hai, toh sabse upar wali string (skinniest) hoti hai '1st string'. Par jab tu Tab padhta hai, toh sabse upar wali line hoti hai 1st string ki, aur sabse neeche wali line hoti hai 6th string (thickest) ki. Matlab, Tab ka layout exactly guitar ke fretboard ke jaisa hai jaise koi tere saamne guitar pakad ke khada hai aur tujhe dikhata hai. Pakka samajh, confusion mat kar. Numbers Ka Khel Lines toh samajh gaye, ab numbers kya hai inke beech mein? Jab kisi line pe koi number likha hota hai, toh uska matlab hai "is string ko is fret number pe dabao." - Agar 1st string ki line pe '0' likha hai, toh uska matlab hai 1st string ko open bajao (koi fret mat daba). - Agar '3' likha hai, toh 3rd fret pe ungli rakh ke us string ko bajao. - Agar '5' likha hai, toh 5th fret. Ekdum simple. Agar numbers ek ke neeche ek likhe hain (vertical stack), toh unhe ek saath bajana hai. Jaise local train ke dabbe mein log ek saath ghuste hain, waise hi saari strings ek saath bajengi. Aur agar numbers line mein aage-peeche hain, toh ek-ek karke bajane hain. Melody banegi. Takeaway: Tab padhna matlab guitar ke fretboard ka X-ray dekhna. Line ka matlab string, number ka matlab fret. Khatam. Online Kalesh: Naye Gaane Kaise Nikaale? Ab tu soch raha hoga, "Bhai, mujhe woh naye viral gaane ka riff bajana hai, kahan se laun?" Internet pe tabs dhoondhna aasan hai, …

7. Ungleebi Khel: Fingerpicking ki ABCD

Picks chhod apne yaar. Ab waqt hai ungliyon ka jaadu dekhne ka. Ab tak tu guitar ko ekdum 'thakela' tarah se strum kar raha tha—poora haath ghuma ke ek saath saari strings ko pel raha tha. Bhai, woh toh Mumbai local ka fast train hai, ek jagah se doosri jagah pahunch jata hai, par beech ke stations miss kar deta hai. Ab hum precision ka khel khelne wale hain. Fingerpicking. Isme hum ek-ek string ko alag-alag chhedte hain. Ek saath nahi, balki ek-ek karke. Yeh nakhre nahi, pure mechanics hai. Ho jaa taiyaar, hath khule rakh. Ungleebi Khel: Fingerpicking ki ABCD Angootha Chhipa, Ungleebi Aage! Strumming mein tu ek pick ya ek haath se guitar ko thok raha tha. Fingerpicking mein teri paanch ungliyan khelengi. Par ruk, koi bhi paanch ungliyan nahi. Iska ek code hai, ek map hai jise hum "PIMA" bolte hain. Yeh Spanish ke alphabets se aaya hai, par tension mat le, hum isko Mumbai ki gali cricket ke hisaab se samjhenge. Guitar ki strings (1 se 6) tere liye ek pitch ki tarah hai. Ab tera right hand ek cricket team ban chuka hai. Har ungli ka apna fielding position hai. p (Pulgar) = Angootha (Thumb): Yeh tera fast bowler hai. Bhai, angootha bass strings par charge karta hai. Yaani top ki 3 strings—E (6th), A (5th), aur D (4th). Yeh heavy lifting karta hai, rhythm deta hai. Angootha hamesha inhi teen strings ke aas-paas rehna chahiye. i (Índice) = Tarjani (Index finger): Yeh tera point karne wali ungli hai. Iska spot fix hai—G string (3rd). m (Medio) = Madhyama (Middle finger): Yeh sabse lambi hoti hai, toh isko thodi extra responsibility do. B string (2nd) iske under hai. a (Anular) = Nameless (Ring finger): Choti ungli. High E string (1st) iske under aati hai. Yeh sabse patli string hai, toh isko chhote se hi sambhalo. Pinky (chhoti ungli) aur sabse badi ungli? Bhai, woh abhi bench par hain. Hum beginners ke liye, woh do abhi extra baggage hain. Inko hawa mein latakne de, ya guitar body par rest karne de. Force mat kar unpar. Real Life Example: Gali cricket khelta hai kabhi? Angootha tera bowler hai jo seedha batsman (bass string) pe gend dalti hai. Baaki teen ungliyan (i, m, a) tere fielders hain—point, cover aur mid-wicket. Jab tak bowler gend nahi dega, fielders kya karenge? Ek-ek karke gend pakadna hai, ek saath nahi. Are you getting this? Angootha hamesha upar (bass) rahega, baaki teen ungliyan neeche (treble). Aisa ulta-pulta haath mat bana lena jisme angootha 1st string pe jaa raha ho. Nautanki mat kar, map yaad rakh. 8. Aakhri Daav: Pehla Gaana Bajao aur Practice Karo

Ab tera tool kit ready hai. 'Pehla Wanda' se chords pakadne ka funda seekh liya, 'Hath Ghuma Boss' mein rhythm daalne ki machine bana, 'Chakka Maar' se chord transitions pe control pa gaya, aur 'Ungleebi Khel' se ungliyan chalane ki kala aagayi. Ab waqt aagaya hai in sab tukdo ko jodne ka. Abhi tak tu alag-alag components check kar raha tha—jaise engine, body, aur tyres alag-alag jagah padi hui thi. Ab poora gaadi ek jagah jama karke ignition on karni hai. Theory ka dabba band, practical ki duniya mein aate hain. Setlist Taiyaar Kar: Sirf Do-Ta Gaane Pakdo Kal ki cycle wale ki tarah mat ban jo subah ek naya bell lagayega aur shaam ko naya stand. Beginner logon ki sabse badi galti kya hote hai? Pata nahi, aaj kya bajana hai. Woh YouTube pe 10 alag-alag tutorials dekh rahe hain, 20 gaano ke chords save kar rakhe hain, aur end mein ek bhi gaana start se end tak nahi baj paata. Information overload mein doob jaate hain. Tujhe ek rule follow karna hai: Apne setlist mein sirf 2 se 3 gaane rakho. Zyada nahi. Aur in gaano ka selection tere fayde ka hona chahiye. Woh aise gaane hone chahiye jisme chord changes simple hon (mostly open chords like C, G, D, Am, E, A) aur strumming pattern basic ho. Koi aisa gaana mat chun jisme sirf 2 second ke liye ek weird chord aata ho jo tera brain melt kar de. Initial level pe flow maintain karna sabse zyada zaroori hai. Gaana chun, uske chord chart nikal (jo tujhe 'Cipher Padho Bhai' wale chapter se aata hai) aur usko apni screen pe laga ya print karke apne saamne rakh de. Are you getting this? Ek gaana pakdo, uski ekdum x-ray karo. Uske baad hi dusre gaane ki taraf dekhna. Takeaway: Bhookh lagne par thali mein sab kuch daal ke khaane se taste kharab hota hai. Ek dish pakdo, chakh ke kha, phir aage badho. Chord Chart Ka Khel: Map Padhna Seekho Tune 'Cipher Padho Bhai' mein dekha hai ki chord chart kaise padhte hain. Ab waqt hai usko real gaane pe apply karne ka. Gaane ka chord chart tere road map ki tarah hai. Local train ka map dekh, agar tu Dadar se Borivali jana chahta hai toh pata hona chahiye kahan utarna hai aur kahan badalna hai. Waise hi chord chart tujhe batayega ki gaana kahan ghumega aur kahan thehr raha hai. Gaane ke lyrics ke upar jo chords likhe aate hain, unhe apni screen pe dekh.
