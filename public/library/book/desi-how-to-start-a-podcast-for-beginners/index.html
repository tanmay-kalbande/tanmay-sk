1. Apna Scene Set Kar: Niche aur Idea

Sabse pehle ek kadak sach sun le: Tera podcast start hone se pehle hi fail ho chuka hai, kyunki tune socha nahi ki tu kya chiyapa karne wala hai. Aise hi mic utha liya aur bolne baith gaya, toh log tere episodes ko skip karne ke liye bhi time waste nahi karenge. Mic kharidne aur Photoshop wale nange cover art banane se pehle, foundation clear hona zaroori hai. Yeh chapter hai tera Niche, tera Idea, aur tera Audience. Yahan se aage badhega toh poora khel palat jayega. Apna Aukaat Aur Pasand Pehchaan (Niche Selection) Dekh, sabse bada trap yeh hai ki "main sab kuch baat karunga". Tech se leke tarot card reading tak, sab cover karunga. Bhai, tu Akshay Kumar thodi hai ki har genre mein aake chala jaayega. Aisa karne wale log wahi hote hain jo local train mein bina ticket chadhte hain—kabhi pakde jaayenge, kabhi nahi, lekin destination tak pahunchna namumkin hai. Niche matlab ek specific ground jahan tu khelna chahta hai. Agar tu "Fitness" bolta hai, toh woh broad hai. "Weight loss for new moms" bol, toh woh ek pinpointed niche ban gaya. Aisa topic pakdo jisme tujhe interest ho aur thoda dum bhi ho. Kyunki podcasting shuru karne ke pehle 3-4 mahine mein koi sunne nahi wala. Agar tujhe khud apne topic se borei hoti hai, toh tu 5 episode ke baad "Bhai, main busy hoon" bolke gaayab ho jayega. Real Life Example: Soch, tu Mumbai ke traffic mein phasa hua hai. Local train ka time aa raha hai. Tujhe ek podcast chahiye jo tera dhyaan bhatkaye. Agar koi banda "Global Economics" ki bakwaas karega, toh tu so jayega aur station miss kar dega. Par agar same banda "Mumbai ki gali cricket ki kahaniyan" sunaye, toh woh tera scene hai. Tujhe connect hoga. Niche wahi hota hai—apne aas-paas ka environment, tera passion, aur woh gap jo market mein chhuta hua hai. Takeaway: Ek tope se 10 logon ko maarna, aur sniper rifle se ek aadmi ka target lena—dono mein baad wala zyada effective hota hai. Apna scope narrow kar, focus sharp kar. Sunne Wale Ka Mood Mausam Nahi Hota (Audience Understanding) Bhai, tu podcast kiski liye bana raha hai? Apne ego satisfy karne ke liye? Agar haan, toh ruk ja, diary mein likh aur rakh le. Podcast audience ke liye banega, tere mitti ka mahatva dikhane ke liye nahi. Tere sunne wale ka mood kya hai? Woh kab sunta hai? Woh kya expect karta hai? Agar tu "Morning Motivation" podcast bana raha hai aur uska tone rona dhona aur dukh dard wala hai, toh log office jaate hue sunke depression mein aa jayenge. Audience ka expectation samajhna zaroori hai. Woh tujhse kya maangti hai? Knowledge? Entertainment? …

2. Format Ka Khamba: Solo ya Team?

Mic uthane se pehle ye chokhni baat samajh le: Agar foundation bekaar hoga, toh podcast bhi local train ke bina signal wale dabbe jaisa lagega—bina engine ke, aage-peeche dhakke khaata hua. Episode ka structure aur tera format, yehi tera engine hai. Aaj hum is engine ko chalu karenge, bina kisi jhanjhat ke. Akele Karega Ya Sath? Format Ka Khel Pehla sawal jo har naye bande ko aata hai: "Bhai, solo jaun ya kisi ko jod lun?" Dekh, iska jawab teri personality par hai. Main dono ka faayda aur nuksaan seedha rakh raha hoon. Solo Format: Tu raja, tu praja, tu mantri. Poora control tere haath mein. Tu kab bolna hai, kab rukna hai, kab gussa karna hai, sab teri marzi. Par iska ek bada kalesh hai. Mic ke saamne akele baith kar bolna aadmi ko pagal bana sakta hai. Shuruati 5 minute mein tujhe lagega jaise shakal se baat kar raha hoon. Par yahan confidence khelta hai. Agar tera content solid hai aur tu apne thoughts ko clearly rakh sakta hai, toh solo ja. Joe Rogan ke solo episodes dekh, bhai ek aadki 3 ghante chala sakta hai. Co-Host Format: Do bande, do awaazein, zyada maza. Co-host tab perfect hai jab tu akele bore ho jata hai ya tujhe kisi ke saath bakchodi karne ki aadat hai. Do logon ki energy alag hi level ki hoti hai. Par idhar bhi problem hai. Agar tera co-host episode ke din bhang ke nasha mein aagaya ya usne padhai hi nahi ki, toh tera episode bacche ki tarah rone lagega. Tujhe uska schedule manage karna padega. Aur haan, beech mein bolne ka chakkar toh laga hi rehta hai. Interview Format: Tere show mein koi aaye, uski story suno. Yeh format tab chalunga jab tera network thoda strong ho. Naye bande seedha big guest ko bula lete hain, bhai pehle koi aam insaan se shuru kar. Interview format mein tera kaam sirf sawaal poochna nahi hai, tera kaam guest se uska best nikalwana hai. Agar tu chup-chap baith ke notes dekhega, toh log podcast sunenge ya school ki history class attend kar rahe hain? Deshi Example: Gali cricket khel rahe ho. Solo format woh hai jahan tu akele batting kar raha hai, poora focus tera shot. Co-host woh hai jahan tera dost bowling kar raha aur tu batting, dono ka jugalbandi chal raha hai. Interview woh hai jahan tu commentary kar raha hai aur koi do match khel raha hai. Samajh gaya scale? Takeaway: Format choose karna apni aukaat aur style ke hisaab se karo. Akele aukaat nahi, toh dost ko jodo. Par yaad rakh, mic ke peeche ka performance teri zimmadaari hai. Episode Ka Khamba: Intro, Main, Outro Agar tu soch raha hai …

3. Samaan Laaya Jaaye: Budget Mic aur Tech

Mic pe bolne ki aukaat tab aati hai jab uske piche laga system teri aawaz ko bajne layak banata hai. Pehle din hi 50,000 ke keechad mic aur 30,000 ke headphones laal karke udhaar karna is the fastest way to ensure your podcast dies before episode 3. Aaj hum todenge ki kam paisa mein kaise pro-level tech khada karte hain. Chalo, machine kholte hain. Mic Ka Khel: USB Ya XLR? Bhai Kaun Sa Khareede? Market mein aaye toh dikhega ki har banda tujhe alag type ka mic bech raha hai. Par sach ye hai ki shuruaat mein tujhe sirf do options samajhne hain: USB aur XLR. Baaki sab bakwas hai. USB Mic: Plug and Play Raja USB mic wo hota hai jo direct tere laptop ya PC ke USB port mein lagta hai. Koi extra machine, koi jhanjhat nahi. Aise soch: local train mein general dabbe mein chad gaya. Seedha entry, seedha travel. Theek thaak quality milegi, par setup ka tension zero hai. Agar tu solo podcast kar raha hai, ya apne pehle 10 episodes ke liye sirf apni aawaz record karna chahta hai, toh USB mic hi tera best dost hai. Yeh tujhe audio interface ya mixer jalebi se bachata hai. Pakka confirm kar lena, par market mein generally ₹5,000 se ₹8,000 ke beech ek decent USB mic mil jata hai jo beginner ke liye kaafi solid hai. Takeaway: USB mic is the "Vada Pav" of podcasting—sasta, pet bhare, aur shuruwaat ke liye best. XLR Mic: Pro Player Ka Tool XLR mic wo hota hai jismein teen pin wala gaada cable hota hai. Par ye direct laptop mein nahi chalega. Iske liye tujhe ek "Audio Interface" ya "Mixer" lena padega—ek aisi machine jo analog signal ko digital mein badalti hai. Ye setup thoda mehenga hota hai aur thoda zyada technical hai. XLR tab lene ka faayda hai jab tu future mein multiple logon ko ek saath record karna chahta hai, ya phir tera setup bohot high-end ho gaya hai. XLR mic aur interface ka combo tujhe easily ₹15,000 se ₹20,000 ke aas-paas peega. Agar tu abhi pehla din hai aur mic pe bolna seekh raha hai, toh XLR pe mat jaa. Pehle USB pe haath saaf kar. Samjha kya? Takeaway: XLR mat kharid abhi, warna audio interface ki wiring mein atak ke podcast chhodna padega. Condenser vs Dynamic: Aawaz Ka Bhed Yahan ek aur chakkar hai. Mic do type ke hote hain: Condenser aur Dynamic. Condenser Mic: Ye bohot sensitive hota hai. Iska kaam hai kamre ke har aawaz ko pakadna. Agar tera kamra aawaz proof hai (acoustics lajawab hain), tabhi ye lena. Warna pankha ki awaaz, kutton ki bhonkne ki aawaz, sab record ho jayegi. Yeh studio mic …

4. Awaaz Pehchaan: Recording aur Acoustics

Mic utha liya, record button daba diya aur khud ko next big podcaster samajh baitha. Par jab wapas suni awaaz, toh laga jaise kisi vacuum cleaner ke andar baith ke bol rahe ho. Yeh kya bakchodi hai? Awaaz record karna sirf 'Play' button dabane ka khel nahi hai. Agar teri recording andar se khokhli hai, toh bahar ka microphone kitna bhi mehenga ho, koi farak nahi padega. Audience ka kan bohot chalu hota hai. Wo content se zyada tere mic pe aane wale "Phusss" aur "Bammm" sunegi. Isliye, is chapter mein hum clean audio record karne ki aukaat banayenge. Mic Se Doori: Pappu Pass Ho Gaya Bhai, mic ko muh se chipka ke nahi bolna hai. Yeh koi tinder date nahi hai jispe tu chipak ke baithna chahta hai. Bahut saare fresh creators ko lagta hai ki mic ke paas hona se awaaz bright aayegi. Haddi todh galat soch. Agar tu condenser mic use kar raha hai (jo bohot sensitive hota hai), toh usse 4 se 6 inch ki doori maintain kar. Ek fist ka gap chhod. Agar dynamic mic hai (jo street survival type hota hai), toh 2 se 3 inch paas aa sakta hai. Desi Example: Local train ki bheed dekhi hai? Jab station aata hai, toh log darwaze pe chipak jaate hain. Wahi galti mic ke saath mat karna. Mic ko space de. Agar tu chipkega, toh teri saansein (breathing) aur muh ki awaaz direct capsule pe aayegi. Phir tu chup-chap ke sun lega apni recording mein "fus-fus fus-fus". Distance maintain karne ka ek solid tareeka hai: Pinky finger ka rule. Apni pinky (choti ungli) mic ke pop filter se lagao aur thumb apne chin pe. Ise 'Pinky-to-Chin' rule bolte hain. Isse tera distance hamesha consistent rahega, chahe tu kitne bhi emotional hoke podcast kare. Takeaway: Mic ka distance nakhre nahi, physics hai. Door sahi, toh awaaz aasmaan. Saans Ka Saamp: Fuse Mat Ho "Main toh ek hi dam mein bolunga, saans toh tab lunga jab mera man kare." Aisi soch rakhne wale log podcast mein atak jaate hain. Jab tu record button dabaata hai, toh adrenaline high hota hai. Tu jaldi jaldi bolne lagta hai aur beech mein saans lene ki bhool jaata hai. Result? Awaaz kaanpne lagti hai, phat-ti hai, aur sunne wale ko suffocation feel hoti hai. Breath control ek muscle hai. Tu jab bol, toh diaphragm (pet ke upar wala hissa) use karna. Saansein naak se le, muh se nahi. Naak se lene se teri awaaz dry nahi hogi aur mic pe thook ya moisture ka attack kam hoga. Analogy: Cricket dekha hai? Jab Virat Kohli pressure mein hota hai, toh wo gali cricket ki tarah har ball pe swing nahi karta. Wo …

5. Aadaa Se Edit: Audio Polish aur Cuts

Recording band ki? Mic ki dust cover oodh diya? Achha. Ab tu soch raha hoga ki bhaiya ji ne 45 minute ki bak-bak ki hai, bas upload kar dunga Spotify pe aur audience pagal ho jayegi. Aise chodega kya? Bina edit kiya hua raw audio direct audience ke kaano mein daalne se acha hai, tu local train ke engine ke neeche apna sar rakh de. 45 minute ki recording mein 10 minute aisi hoti hai jahan tu "umm", "aaa", "matlab" bol raha hota hai, ya pichle scene mein bhaukal ki galti kar di hai. Yeh sab cut karna, polish karna—yahi farq hai "kaccha aam" aur "sasta nashebaaz" ke beech. Audio editing tere podcast ka makeup room hai. Yahan se tu ek average recorder se ek professional broadcaster ban sakta hai, warna reh tu wahi rahega jo apne mobile pe unfiltered bakwas karta hai. Chalo, DAW (Digital Audio Workstation—bas fancy naam hai editing software ka) khol aur dhyan se sun. Kachra Nikalo: Awkward Silences aur 'Umm' Ka Safaya Sun, log tujhse sikhne ya relatable hona chahte hain, unka data waste karne ka mazaak nahi le sakta. Jab tu bolte hue rukta hai, "umm" karta hai, ya mic pe saans chodta hai... yeh sab cheezein sunne wale ke kaan ki dewaani kar deti hain. Pehla kaam: Awkward silences ko hunt kar. Har ek DAW (Audacity, GarageBand, Reaper) mein ek feature hota hai jo "Silence Finder" ya "Noise Gate" kehta hai. Iska kaam kya hai? Tere bolne ke beech ke khaali hisse ko detect karta hai. Lekin dhyan se sun—har khaali jagah ko delete mat kar. Natural breathing zaroori hai. Agar tu machine ban gaya na, toh sunne wale ko lagega AI bol raha hai. Dusra aur sabse bada kachra: "Umm", "Aaa", "Matlab", "You know". Yeh filler words tere confidence ki dhajjiyan udate hain. Pehli baar editing karte waqt tujhe lagega bhai, mere toh 100 'umm' aa gaye 10 minute mein. Tension mat le. Sabko select kar aur 'Delete' daba. Dhyan rakh: Jab tu ek sentence beech mein kaat raha hai, toh thoda sa crossfade laga de. Seedha cut karoge toh awaaz "click" karegi, jaise kisi ne tooti hui bottle ka thook diya ho. Crossfade se cut smooth ho jata hai. Bina iske tera podcast gali ki cricket match ki tarah lagega jahan har ball pe koi na koi chilla raha ho. Takeaway: Kachra ghar mein rakha toh mehman bhagenge. Filler words aur dead air ko kaat, par natural breathing ko zinda rakh. Kaano Ka Massage: Noise Reduction aur EQ Ka Khel Awaaz toh record ho gayi, par sunne mein aisi lag rahi hai jaise kisi ne lohe ka bartan ragad raha ho. Background ka fan, cooler, ya bahar ki traffic—sab kuch mic …

6. Naam Kya Rakha?: Branding aur Cover Art

Tere paas mic hai, audio edit ho gaya, par ab tak tere show ka koi naam nahi hai. Bina naam ke duniya mein ghoomta toh koi nahi dekhta, aur podcast bina naam aur cover ke bas ek MP3 file hai jise koi double click nahi karega. Aaj hum banayenge teri podcast ki pehchaan. Naam Soch, Dimaag Khol Sabse pehla step: Tere show ko log kya bulayenge? "Mera Podcast" ya "Episode 1" rakhne se tera show kahin nahi jaayega. Naam aisa hona chahiye jo search bar mein seedha chhapke aaye aur dimag mein chipak jaaye. Soch, tu local train mein khada hai. Bheed itni ki saans lena bhi permission maangni padti hai. Tabhi koi aake bolega "Bhai, suna kya?" Tu puchega "Kya?" Agar woh bolega "Mera naya show aaya hai," toh tera reaction kya hoga? Bhai, tu apna earphone adjust karke usko ignore kar dega. Par agar woh bolega "Bhai, 'Chai Pe Charcha' suna? Aaj episode aaya hai traffic ka," toh shayad tu ruk ke sune. Yehi power hai naam ki. Tere show ka naam tera pehla hook hai. Apple Podcasts aur Spotify ke algorithm ko search queries chahiye. Agar tera naam "Rahul Ki Duniya" hai, toh log "Rahul" search karne wale nahi hain. Log "startup tips", "comedy hindi", ya "horror stories" search karte hain. Agar tere naam mein woh search keyword nahi hai, toh tu dark mein kho jaayega. Title ke 3 Formula: 1. Clear aur Descriptive: "The Tech Guide Hindi" ya "Startup Stories India". Seedha point. Algorithm isko pakad leta hai. 2. Catchy + Keyword: "Filmy Charcha: Bollywood Reviews" ya "Gully Cricket Tales". Yahan catchy word aur search keyword dono hain. 3. Personal Brand + Niche: "Amit ki Diet Plan" ya "Neha talks Stocks". Agar tera pehle se kuch audience hai, toh yeh chal jayega. Par beginner ke liye, pehla aur doosra formula best hai. Bada mat ban, dimag mat kha. Naam chota rakh, spell karne mein aasaan ho, aur bolne mein smooth ho. Lamba aur twisted naam log type nahi karenge. Takeaway Naam rakhne mein over-smart mat ban. Search-friendly aur catchy naam hi woh hai jo tere show ko search engine ki galiyon se bahar nikalega. Description: Algorithm Ka Khana Naam set kar liya? Ab us naam ko describe kar. Yeh description tere show ka CV hai. Jab koi tere podcast ke page par aayega, toh yeh description padh ke decide karega ki sunni hai ya nahi. Aur sabse bada, yeh description Apple aur Spotify ke algorithm ko batayega ki tera show kis baare mein hai. Maan le tu dating app par hai. Tere paas sirf 3 second hain kisi ko impress karne ke liye. Agar tere bio mein likha hai "Achha ladka hoon," toh koi swipe right …

7. Bhej Dunga Boss: Hosting aur Distribution

File edit ho gayi, awaaz crisp aa gayi, cover art ekdum tight hai. Ab tak tu apne PC ke andar bhaith ke maharaja ban raha hai. Par sach sach bata, teri recording abhi tak ek kaidi hai jo lockup mein baithi hai. Duniya tak pahunchna hai? Isko bahar nikalna padega. Agar tu soch raha hai ki MP3 file direct YouTube ya Spotify pe daal dega, toh band kar de ye sapne. Aisa nahi hota. Audio internet pe lagaane ka ek system hai, ek protocol hai. Jo samajh gaya, woh baazi maarega. Jo nahi samjha, woh apne hard drive mein hi podcast sunta rahega. Chalo, is lockup ka gate kholte hain. Makan Maalik Yaad Se: Hosting Platform Ka Khel Teri pasand ka mic, tera edit software, tera voice—ye sab toh bangla hai. Par ye bangla zameen pe khada hona chahiye, hawa mein nahi. Zameen matlab hosting platform. Dekh, jab tu YouTube pe video daalta hai, toh YouTube tera server hai. Wo teri video ka data apne supercomputers mein rakhta hai. Par podcasting alag khel hai. Apple Podcasts ya Spotify aukaat nahi rakhte sabhi podcasters ki audio files apne servers pe store karne ki. Unke paas space ki problem hai. Isliye, tujhe ek "Podcast Host" rent pe lena padta hai. Ye basically ek makan maalik hai. Tu isko apni audio file (MP3) deta hai, aur wo usko apne server pe rakh leta hai. Phir jab koi sun-na-wala Spotify pe tera show dhundega, toh Spotify us host ke server se link maang ke chala jayega. Desi Example: Tu local train mein hai. Tere paas vada pav hai (teri audio file). Par khaane ke liye plate chahiye (hosting platform). Vada pav plate pe aayega, tabhi tu chalti train mein khaayega. Direct haath se khaayega toh laaldi lag jayegi. Server down, link break, sab band. Bade Khiladi Kaun Hain? Market mein bohot saare hosts available hain. Buzzsprout, Libsyn, Podbean, Anchor. Panga mat le, seedha scene samajh. Pehle ek app tha "Anchor". Usko Spotify ne kharid liya. Ab iska naam hai Spotify for Podcasters. Beginner ke liye ye ekdum amrit hai. Free hai, unlimited hosting deta hai, aur sabse important baat—Spotify ki direct integration milti hai. Tera show turant Spotify pe jata hai. Buzzsprout aur Libsyn paid hain, lekin unka analytics aur distribution network thoda pro-level ka hai. Jab tera show bada hoga, tab unke paas shift kar lena. Abhi shuruat mein, paise mat bacha kisi cheez pe jo free aur reliable mil rahi hai. Par ek baat pakad lo—jo host tera RSS feed generate kare, wahi tera asli boss hai. Are you getting this? Punchline: Hosting pe paisa bachaane ki jagah nahi, par free aur solid platform pe apna base banana hi sabse cool faisle …

8. Entry Maara: Launch aur Promotion

Recording band, edit master-piece ready, aur distribution platforms pe bhi sab set hai. Ab bani hai baas woh button dabaane ki jo tujhe internet ke map pe chhap lagaa de. Pehle din ki entry aisi honi chahiye ki log notice karne par majboor ho jaayein. Ekdum local train ke peak hour mein gate kholne jaisi impact wali. Pehli Bike Ka Teer: Trailer Ka Khamba Direct episode 1 seedha platform pe phenkne ki jaldi mat kar. Audience ko achanak se 45 minute ka audio ghoosne se pehle, unhe ek chota sa 'trailer' do. Yeh trailer tera teaser hai, tera hook hai, tera first impression hai. Trailer 30 se 60 second ka hona chahiye. Isme poora khandaan mat bhar. Sirf teen cheezein: 1. Tu kaun hai? 2. Yeh show kiske baare mein hai? 3. Audience ko kya milega? Isko soch ek movie trailer jaisa. Jab 'KGF' ya 'Pathaan' ka trailer aata hai, toh puri kahani thodi batate hain? Bas vibe, action aur ek punchline dete hain. Tera podcast trailer bhi exactly yahi karna chahiye. Apple Podcasts aur Spotify dono pe 'Trailer' tag alag se hota hai. Isse algorithm samajh jaata hai ki bhai yeh toh shuruaat hai, isko naye logon ke feed mein push karo. Desi Example: Soch tere area ka naya Vada Pav stall khula hai. Tu kya sabse pehle unhe ek poora plate khilayega? Nahi. Tu ek chota sa bite dega, taste karayega, aur bola "Agar aisi hai toh poori plate le lo." Trailer tera woh free ka taste hai. Isme apni aawaaz ka confidence, tera music, aur tera content ka core flavor hona chahiye. Takeaway: Trailer tere show ki aukaat hai. Bina iske launch karne ka matlab hai bina kapde ke shaadi mein jaana. Bheed Todne Ka Formula: Day 1 Pe Teer Baaje Beginners ki sabse badi galti yeh hoti hai ki woh launch day pe sirf ek episode daalte hain. Ek episode daala, fir 3 din wait kiya ki "Yaar, downloads kyun nahi aa rahe?" Bhai, internet pe attention span bohot chhota hai. Aaj kal ke zamaane mein log jab koi naya show dekhte hain, toh unhe binge karna pasand hai. Ek episode dekhke unka mood nahi banta, woh sochte hain "Chal jab 5-6 aa jaye tabhi dekhenge." Isliye, Day 1 pe seedha 3 episodes drop kar. Kyun? Kyunki jab koi tere trailer se impress hoke tere show pe aayega, toh usko ek episode sunte hi bhookh lagni chahiye doosre ke liye. Agar uske paas 2 aur episodes ready mil gaye, toh woh ek hi baar mein teeno sun lega. Algorithm bhi dekhega ki "Arre, log is podcast ko 1 ghanta non-stop sun rahe hain, yeh toh kamaal ka content hai!" aur teri reach automatically badh jayegi. Analogy: …

9. Khel Chalu Rahega: Consistency aur Paisa

Launch ka hangover ab utar gaya hoga? Pehle episode daala, 50 logon ne sun liya, 10 ne share kiya, aur ab tu soch raha hai "Bhai, main toh next big podcast star ban gaya." Ruk ja. Ek episode se koi star nahi banta. Asli khel toh ab shuru hota hai. Launch sirf trailer tha, picture abhi baaki hai mere dost. Agar tu soch raha hai ki ek episode daal ke baith jaunga aur brands line mein laggenge apne paisa chhatakane, toh tu bada bholey hai. Podcasting ek marathon hai, 100-meter dash nahi. Jo bhag ke aage nikal jaata hai, woh thak ke rasta nahi badalta. Jo apni aukaat aur pace mein chalta hai, woh final line pe aata hai. Samjha kya? Chal, ab dekhte hain is khel ko lamba kaise chalana hai aur kab paise ka ped ugna shuru hoga. Aag Rakho, Par Jala Mat: Content Calendar Ka Jugaad Naya podcaster hota kya hai? Pehle hafte 5 episodes record karta hai, energy aasman pe hoti hai. Doosre hafte 2 record karta hai. Teesre hafte "Bhai, ideas nahi aa rahe, thoda break le raha hoon creative burnout se." Aisi bakchodi mat kar. Tu local train ki tarah hona chahiye—bhaap toh nikalti hai, shor toh karti hai, par time pe aati hai. Ek din late hui, log aag lagane lagte hain. Consistency is your only currency right now. Spotify aur Apple ke algorithms tere pehle 10-15 episodes dekhte hain. Agar unhe laga schedule pakka hai, toh woh tujhe push karenge. Nahi toh kachre mein daal denge. Content Calendar Banana Kya Hota Hai? Calendar banane ka matlab yeh nahi ki tu corporate Excel sheet bana raha hai. Iska matlab hai tu apne dimag ka kachra pehle hi saaf kar raha hai. Deshi example le: Tu Mumbai local mein travel kar raha hai.
