1. Introduction to Selling on Etsy: What You Need to Know

What is Etsy—and Why Should You Care? Imagine waking up to a notification that someone on the other side of the world just bought your handmade ceramic mug. They paid with a tap of their phone, and within days, your order is packed and shipped, all without leaving your home. That’s the magic of Etsy—a global online marketplace where creative people sell unique, handcrafted, vintage, or custom items to millions of buyers who value individuality over mass-produced goods. Etsy isn’t the only place to sell online (think Amazon, eBay, or Shopify), but it’s uniquely designed for artists, crafters, designers, and small makers. Unlike Amazon, which sells everything from toothpaste to TVs, Etsy is a curated “digital craft fair” where the focus is on creativity, storytelling, and personal connection. It’s a place where your passion project can become a business—and where customers come specifically to discover something new, not just to buy a replacement for something broken. So, is Etsy right for you? Let’s find out. --- What Exactly Is Etsy? Etsy is an online marketplace founded in 2005 that connects sellers (people like you who create or curate products) with buyers who are looking for unique, handmade, vintage, or custom items. Think of it as a virtual craft fair that’s open 24/7, 365 days a year. Key Features of Etsy: - Handmade Focus: Etsy encourages original, one-of-a-kind items. Most sellers create their own products, though some curate vintage finds (20+ years old) or craft supplies. - Custom Orders: Many Etsy sellers offer personalized or made-to-order items—like engraved jewelry, custom illustrations, or tailored wedding invitations. - Community-Driven: Etsy has a strong sense of community. Sellers share tips, buyers leave reviews, and the platform values artistry and authenticity. - Global Reach: Over 90 million active buyers in nearly every country can discover your shop. Etsy handles payments in multiple currencies and manages international shipping logistics. Unlike platforms like Amazon or eBay, where you’re competing with big brands and endless generic products, Etsy is a place where your unique story matters. Buyers come to Etsy not just to shop—they come to experience something personal. That’s a powerful advantage for beginners. --- How Is Etsy Different From Other Marketplaces? It’s not just a type of product—like “jewelry” or “home décor”—but a specific version of it that appeals to a particular group of buyers. Unlike Amazon or eBay, where generic products dominate, Etsy buyers often search for handmade, custom, or one-of-a-kind items. Transaction Fees: Etsy’s Cut of Your Sale - What it is: Etsy takes 6.5% of the total sale price, including the cost of the item and any shipping you charge the buyer. - How it works: - This fee is deducted automatically when you make a sale. - It applies to the total amount the buyer pays, including shipping (if you charge it separately). - Example: If you sell a $50 necklace and charge $5 for shipping, Etsy takes 6.5% of $55 = $3.58. 3.
